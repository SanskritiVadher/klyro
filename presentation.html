RULE 1: INTEGER PAISE

Every monetary column is 64-bit BIGINT paise. ₹500 is stored as 50000. Floats are banned.

RULE 2: ATOMIC TRANSFERS

Moving money generates two synchronized rows sharing a UUID. Both commit together or roll back.

RULE 3: RESERVE SHIELD

is_reserve = True marks emergency funds, programmatically excluding them from spendable cash.
